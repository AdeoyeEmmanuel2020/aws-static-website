## **Key Architecture Decisions**

- **Serverless Architecture:** Eliminated operational overhead by adopting serverless S3 hosting, reducing management complexity and enabling focus on business logic rather than infrastructure maintenance
- **Global Content Delivery:** Selected CloudFront for <200ms worldwide latency
- **Security-First Design:** Enforced HTTPS-only traffic with ACM certificates
- **Cost-Optimised Solution:** Leveraged AWS free tier and pay-per-use pricing model
- **Infrastructure as Code:** Choose Terraform for reproducible, version-controlled deployments
---
